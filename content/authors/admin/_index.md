Vittoria De Pellegrini is a PhD student in Earth Science and Engineering at KAUST and a Doctoral Researcher with the DeepWave Research Consortium. She builds deep generative AI and video diffusion systems for subsurface reservoir characterization, running CUDA-aware, reproducible workflows on the KAUST IBEX supercomputing cluster. Vittoria combines geoscience expertise with advanced machine learning to forecast CO2 monitoring, well-log predictions, and complex fluid-flow dynamics while sharing research through open science and community collaborations.
